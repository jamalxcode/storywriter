      Your privacy

      This site has no server and no database. It never stores your video, your stories or your account anywhere online. Nothing is sent back to this website.

      • Stays on your device
        Your video and its sound. They're processed in your browser and never uploaded. The speech is turned into text on your device, too.
      • Sent to the AI
        Still frames and the speech transcript. When you press "Write the story", these are sent to the AI (Claude, made by Anthropic) through OpenRouter, so it can write about them. Their privacy policies cover how they handle it.
      • Stays on your device
        Your story and the AI's notes. Shown on this page only and gone when you close or reload it. Use Copy or Download to keep a story.
      • Saved in this browser
        Your OpenRouter sign-in and your settings. Kept so you stay signed in. "Sign out" removes them.

      The page also loads its fonts from Google Fonts, its speech model from Hugging Face and jsDelivr, and the YouTube player when you use a YouTube link. Like any website, these services see your IP address, but they receive none of your content. AI use is billed to your own OpenRouter account.
